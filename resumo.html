♠ Simple Present

Rotinas e hábitos (p. 6 e p. 46)

+I play tennis. · He plays tennis. · They play tennis.
–I don't play tennis. · He doesn't play tennis. · He doesn't cheat.
?Do you play tennis? Yes, I do. No, I don't.
Does he play tennis? Yes, he does. No, he doesn't.
Do they usually win?
📌 Dica de prova: he / she / it → verbo com -s ou -es. Mas depois de doesn't e Does o -s some: He doesn't play · Does she play?

Adverbs of frequency (p. 6)

100% always → usually → often → sometimes → hardly ever → never 0%
  • Vão antes do verbo: We always study hard. I often beat him.
  • Mas depois do verbo to be: She is never late.

Time expressions (p. 6)

now · every day · at the moment · twice a week · on the weekend · this year · once a week

Costumam ir no fim da frase, mas podem ir no começo: We usually chat online twice a week. On Mondays I do volunteer work.

♥ Present Progressive e o -ing

O que está acontecendo agora (p. 6 e p. 46)

+I'm drawing. · She's drawing. · They're drawing.
–I'm not drawing. · He isn't drawing. · You aren't drawing.
?Is he drawing? Yes, he is. No, he isn't. · Are they losing?
⚠️ Pegadinha: são duas partes obrigatórias — am/is/are + verbo -ing. “He drawing” e “He is draw” estão errados.

Tense review: rotina × agora (p. 46)

simple presentpresent progressive
+I often beat him.I'm beating him.
–He doesn't cheat.She isn't winning.
?Do they usually win?Are they losing?
📌 Ache a pista: always / on Saturdays / every day → simple present. now / at the moment / today → present progressive.

Gerunds (p. 6)

  • Depois de verbos de gostar / não gostar: He likes playing tennis. She hates going skydiving.
  • Depois de preposições: I'm into drawing cartoons. They're interested in reading books. He's good at playing the piano.
⚠️ Pegadinha: o gerund NÃO é present progressive. Em He likes playing tennis, o verbo principal é likes.

♣ Simple Past

Verbos regulares: a mesma forma para todos (p. 18)

I traveled around Australia. · She traveled around Australia. · They traveled around Australia.

Regra de spellingExemplo

terminado em -e: + -darrive → arrived

Verbos irregulares: lista própria no livro (p. 52).

Negativa, pergunta e pistas (p. 46)

+It appeared in 1475. · We played cards last Saturday.
–He didn't play Go.
?Did she win?

Pistas: in 1989 · yesterday · 5,000 years ago · last week

📌 Dica de prova: depois de didn't e de Did o verbo volta à forma base: didn't play · Did she win?

♦ Past Progressive

O que estava acontecendo (p. 30 e p. 46)

+I was listening. · She was listening. · You were listening. · They were listening.
–I wasn't listening. · We weren't listening. · He was not running.
?Was he walking? Yes, he was. No, he wasn't.
Were you walking? Yes, you were. No, you weren't.
What were you doing at 9pm on Monday?
  • was → I, he, she, it · were → you, we, they
  • Resposta curta sem repetir o -ing: Were you running at 6pm? Yes, I was.
  • Pistas: yesterday at 9pm · last night at 3am — I wasn't reading a book yesterday at 9pm.

♣ + ♦ juntos: when e while (p. 30 e p. 46)

I was running to catch the bus when I fell.

  • past progressive = a atividade em andamento (I was running to catch the bus)
  • simple past = a ação que interrompeu (when I fell)
  • when antes do simple past: He was carrying the box when he dropped it.
  • while antes do past progressive: While he was carrying the box, he dropped it.
📌 Macete: WHILE gruda no pedaço com was/were + -ing. WHEN gruda na ação curta do simple past.

📚 Glossário dos termos

base form: o verbo “puro” (play, watch).
-ing: a terminação do present/past progressive e do gerund.
adverbs of frequency: palavras de frequência (always … never).
time expressions: expressões de tempo (now, yesterday, twice a week…).
gerund: verbo com -ing depois de like/hate e de preposições.
spelling: o jeito de escrever o verbo com -ed.
short answer: resposta curta (Yes, I do. / No, she wasn't.).
tense review: revisão dos tempos verbais (Unit 8, p. 46).
